### 🌤️ About Me

I'm a Product Designer and frontend/mobile engineer based in Alexandria, Egypt, and a recent Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence graduate from Pharos University. I love owning the full pipeline of a product — from initial research, wireframes, and high-fidelity prototypes, right through to the actual React or Flutter build that ships to users.

**A few things I care about:**
- 📊 Grounding interface decisions in real user data, not assumptions.
- ♿ Accessible, WCAG-compliant experiences by default, not as an afterthought.
- 🤝 Design-to-development handoffs that don't lose fidelity along the way.
